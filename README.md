👥 HR Analytics & Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to help the HR Department of Afaq Investment Company evaluate employee performance, analyze salary distribution, and gain insights into workforce demographics and geographic presence.

🧠 Problem Statement

The HR department aims to:

Evaluate performance across different departments and managers.

Understand salary allocation and average compensation across the organization.

Analyze geographic employee distribution to optimize regional operations.

Identify key trends in employee experience and tenure.

❓ Business Question

What are the key drivers of performance within the company, and how can we optimize salary structures, experience levels, and regional presence to improve overall efficiency?

🔧 Data Transformations

📌 Performance Rating Analysis

Employees were categorized by their Performance Rating (P.Rating) to assess efficiency.

Evaluated average ratings by manager to ensure consistent performance standards.

📌 Experience & Age Metrics

Calculated the average years of experience (4.3 years) and average age (48.3 years) of the workforce.

Mapped these metrics against P.Ratings to identify correlations between tenure and performance.

📄 Dashboard Pages

The Power BI report includes:

Summary: High-level KPIs including total employees (702), total salary (SAR 25.1M), and department counts.

Department & Manager Analysis: Detailed breakdown of average performance ratings by department and manager.

Geographic Insights: Visual mapping of employee distribution across 12 cities.

Demographics: Breakdown by gender (Male/Female) and nationality.
